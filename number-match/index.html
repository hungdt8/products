The rules

Everything clears in pairs

A pair only clears when the two numbers can reach each other — in a row, a column, on a diagonal, or wrapping around to the next line. Anything already crossed out doesn't block the way.

Three ways to play

Same rules, different boards

All three modes follow the same rules above — what changes is the board you're given to start from.

A new board every stage, tuned to the difficulty you choose — Basic, Medium or Hard. Clear one and the next deals, a little longer and a little meaner.

★ stars every stage

Classic Board

The pen-and-paper opening: 1 to 19, written a digit at a time. The same board every time, and a kind one to learn on.

One puzzle a day, identical for every player. Solve it to keep your streak going.
