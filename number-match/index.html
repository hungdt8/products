Support

Questions, a page you're stuck on, or a purchase that didn't come through? Write to support@hanminstudio.online. In the app, Settings › Contact Support fills in your app version and device for you.

Bought the full version on another device? Open any locked chapter and tap Restore Purchase.

Privacy

Inkten doesn't collect, store or share any personal data.

  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking and no analytics.
  • Your progress, settings and statistics are saved only on your device.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details.
  • The only network request is the optional "Discover More Apps" list in Settings, which downloads a public list of our apps and sends nothing about you.
