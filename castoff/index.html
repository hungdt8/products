The puzzle where the water moves.

Every board starts as a tangle. Then the tide turns. Which line can leave first, and can it leave right now? Try this board and find out.

Low tide

Tap an arrow with a clear run. Sand stops arrows at low tide, and the tide turns every 3 arrows.

The tide changes the board

Most puzzles ask what's free. Castoff also asks when. The same arrow can be stuck one moment and clear the next.

Low tide

Sandbars rise out of the water and stop any arrow that reaches them. A bump costs a heart.

High tide

The sand slips under the water and the way is open. The tide turns every time 3 arrows leave, so your order decides what opens next.

Wait, if you must

Waiting turns the tide for the price of a flag, never a heart, so you can always finish. Find the right order and you never need to.
